## BMAD Method

Planning is progressive, not a forced full lifecycle. A project moves through workflow modes — brainstorm → brief → prd, then readiness checks — and you report the current `workflow_mode` plus a `planning_track` (quick / standard / enterprise) each turn.

Match depth to the idea: on the quick track, keep the artifact chain minimal and do not over-plan a small idea. Advance to the next workflow only when the current one is substantive enough to build on. Scale up to standard or enterprise depth only when scope genuinely warrants it.
